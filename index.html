Approval workflow

1

Draft

Line Manager fills the form and promotion Yes / No.

2

Send to HOD

Checked for completeness, then locked for the Line Manager.

3

Submitted

HOD reviews, can edit, then approves or returns.

4

Approved

Final and locked. Only HR can reopen.

Complete before sending

At least 4 objectives, weightage 100, none above 30%, all 6 BPI rated, promotion answered.

Returned forms

The HOD note shows on top of the form. Fix and send again.

History

Every send, approval and return is logged with who and when.

Promotions

Step 1: Yes or No

On the assessment form. No is saved as Not Recommended.

Step 2: Details

Yes opens a form. History fills from Master. Add position, grade and key achievements.

Step 3: HR decides

Organogram, remarks, final status. Every decision is logged.
